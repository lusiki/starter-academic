Reputational crises rarely break without warning. What precedes them is a shift in tone, a rise in emotional intensity, a sharpening of polarisation, a sudden acceleration in how fast content spreads, and unusual levels of engagement. CRISP AI tracks those signals in real time and turns them into an early warning.

The system reads online and social-media content alongside live radio and television broadcasts, and builds four things on top of that stream: an AI-based predictive model, a real-time audiovisual analysis module, a "PR crisis fingerprint" classification framework, and an interface that hands the user an early warning together with recommended response actions.

# Revised portfolio direction

Luka Šikić — reviewed 26 September 2026. This replaces the portfolio priorities in `repository-evidence-review.md`. Implemented in the fourth edition of the local preview, with the subsequently supplied Rimac brief added as the featured reputation case. The site remains unpublished.

## Positioning

**Independent media intelligence for communications, brands and public affairs.**

Suggested supporting copy: “I turn complex media coverage into clear evidence about visibility, narratives and reputation—through focused research, transparent methods and reporting built around your question.”

Lead with Luka and the analytical service. DigiKat can remain a brief biography/project credit. Its specialist subject matter and annual-review chart should no longer define the main commercial story. The earlier selection put too much weight on a finished report and too little on relevance to a prospective buyer.

## The three cases to feature

### 1. Media framing: the strongest main visual

**Client question:** How is the story changing around an important policy or event?

Use the euro-adoption study, co-authored with Davor Trbušić and Stela Lechpammer and published in 2026. Its archived analysis covers 236 articles across December 2022 and January 2023. The repository's rendered contingency table supports this compact comparison:

| Frame | December 2022, n=94 | January 2023, n=142 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Technological-administrative | 75 articles / 79.8% | 78 articles / 54.9% |
| Social | 5 articles / 5.3% | 33 articles / 23.2% |

**Proposed headline:** “Beyond mentions: see how the story changes.”

**Visual:** Two pairs of horizontal bars, direct percentage labels, one muted December colour and one lime January colour. The different monthly sample sizes make percentages preferable to raw-count comparisons. State that these are shares within the analysed sample, not estimates for all Croatian reporting. These descriptive results do not establish causation.

**Service connection:** Narrative analysis before, during and after a policy change, launch or controversy; comparisons by outlet and period.

Sources: [published study](https://doi.org/10.34135/communicationtoday.2026.Vol.17.No.1.15), [archived analysis and figures](https://github.com/lusiki/Einfuhrung_des_Euro/tree/1ff02b68e381b5cae1a50ff2a66471c3c66c0084). Percentages above were independently calculated from the archived table. Repository-rendered charts were inspected; the publisher's PDF was not retrieved successfully.

### 2. Branded content: the clearest commercial connection

**Client question:** How do brands structure sponsored stories across publishers?

The 2025 native-advertising paper analyses 543 ads from six Croatian portals, collected in 2021–2022. Its headline categories, sources and industry comparisons support a content audit case. This is completed, co-authored research—not merely a repository proposal.

**Visual:** A compact headline-format distribution, or a selective industry-by-format heatmap with sample sizes. Rebuild from the underlying data rather than reproduce the journal artwork.

**Service connection:** Publisher comparisons, competitor content audits and evidence to inform creative briefs. Avoid causal ROI claims. Some reach-related prose and tables need reconciliation before using an “influencers perform best” sales claim.

Sources: [published paper](https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/clanak/495017), [analysis repository](https://github.com/lusiki/NativeAadvertising).

### 3. Institutional coverage: a recent practical example

**Client question:** Which outlets account for an institution's coverage?

Use HNB Media Attention as a clearly labelled public research draft. The broad media hub documents 33,235 captured publications explicitly mentioning HNB across 281 outlets, January 2021–August 2026. HNB is the subject of research; this does not imply a client relationship.

**Visual:** Five ranked outlet bars plus the remaining sources, showing concentration. A deeper case page can compare all-source trends with a fixed source panel to explain collection effects.

**Service connection:** Institutional visibility, source prioritisation and coverage benchmarking. Keep the draft status visible; the current release indicates that author review and independent validation are pending.

Sources: [media hub](https://lusiki.github.io/HNB_Media_Attention/media/), [versioned evidence](https://github.com/lusiki/HNB_Media_Attention/tree/fd26489896d05b39a3b9e49ca2ea69d1125c4426/media-hub).

## A distinctive supporting direction

[GIMES](https://github.com/lusiki/GIMES_HR-inflacija) provides a more distinctive economics-and-media connection. The repository contains rendered reports and exported indices, so there is executed work to show. A small comparison of inflation-related themes could introduce “Economic narratives in the media.”

Present this as research in development. The inspected inflation report is historical, and its validation does not establish out-of-sample forecasting performance. Avoid a “live early-warning system” claim. A small-multiple display of observed media measures would communicate the research more clearly than a composite-index/official-inflation chart with two vertical axes.

## How this should look on the page

Use the section title **“Questions answered with evidence.”** Give the euro case the main space, followed by smaller branded-content and institutional cases. Each should contain a question, one short finding, one legible chart and a link to the study. Put date range, unit, collaborators and publication status directly beneath the graphic.

Draw charts in SVG/HTML using the page's existing typography and ink/lime palette. Use direct labels, minimal grid lines and restrained animation. The finding should remain readable on mobile without opening the full report. Remove the large pasted annual-report image; another full academic-chart screenshot would reproduce the integration problem.

Then show the practical outputs a commission could produce: an executive brief, an annotated evidence appendix, a publisher comparison and a documented data extract where permitted. Label any newly prepared examples as samples, not past client work.

Keep the Reddit publication as supporting methods credibility. Keep Xiaomi and Rimac as dated brand/event reporting examples further down. Repositories containing only a README or teaching illustrations should not be promoted as completed analytical cases.

The result should feel like a selective portfolio of analytical judgement, with the wider research archive one click away.
